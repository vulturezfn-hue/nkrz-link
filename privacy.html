Privacy Policy

NKRZ Streamer Rewards · Last updated October 13, 2026

This policy explains what NKRZ Streamer Rewards collects when you link your streaming account, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • From TikTok / Twitch / Kick when you sign in: your username, display name and account ID. Nothing else.
  • From the game: your NKRZ game account ID and in-game name, so rewards go to the right player.
  • Reward records: hours streamed, coins earned and when you linked or unlinked.

We do not collect your password, email, videos, followers, messages or any other content. The sign-in token is used once to read your username and is not stored.

How we use it

  • To link your streaming account to your NKRZ game account.
  • To show your channel link when you go live (for example in our Discord).
  • To give out rewards and stop abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Your channel link may be shown publicly in our Discord or in game when you go live, and NKRZ staff can see reward records.

Storage

Data is stored on the NKRZ game server and kept only while your account is linked or as needed for reward records.

Your choices

Unlink any time with /unlinkstream in game or /stream unlink in Discord, and remove the app's access in your TikTok settings. To have your records deleted, ask us in Discord.

Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 13.

Contact

Questions? Reach the NKRZ team in our Discord: discord.gg/nkrz.
